📊 **Decline of a Motor Insurance company (EIC pvt. ltd.) Dashboard — Power BI**

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze sales performance and revenue trends, enabling data-driven decision-making through dynamic visuals, KPIs, and drill-down reporting.

🚀 **Overview**

This project transforms raw data into a set of interactive Power BI reports, providing stakeholders with a clear, at-a-glance view of revenue performance, product/category trends, and regional or customer-level breakdowns.

🛠️ **Tech Stack**

Power BI Desktop — report design & data visualization

Power Query (M) — data extraction, cleaning, and transformation

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) — calculated columns, measures, and KPIs

Data Modeling — star schema relationships between fact and dimension tables

**Summary**

From 2014–2016, the company was collecting only 34–39 cents in premium for every dollar it paid in claims — a massive underwriting loss (roughly $1.5–2bn/year). Things improved through 2017 and flipped positive in 2018

The company spent 2014–2017 paying out far more in claims than it collected in premium, and only turned the corner in 2018.

**Bleeders and saviours.**

1. Trailers and semitrailers: 15% → 28% → 17% → 19% → 33% — consistently the worst-priced segment across all five years, never coming close to break-even even in the "recovery" year. This is the chronic problem child.

2. Automobile, Bus, Pick-up, Station Wagons, Tanker: all start well below 100% (30–75% range in 2014–2016) and steadily climb toward or past 100% by 2017–2018 — this is the broad-based improvement driving the overall turnaround.

3. Motor-cycle: 129% → 152% → 344% → 754% → 1407% — already profitable in 2014 and became extremely profitable. Combined with it being the highest-volume product every year, this segment is effectively carrying the book.

4. Tractor (177%→472%→154%→3226%→1203%) and Special construction (79%→47%→78%→127%→762%): profitable overall, but Tractor's swings (especially that 3226% spike) suggest a very small, lumpy portfolio — a handful of claims (or their absence) can swing the ratio wildly. Worth checking policy counts before trusting that number.




**My recommendations**

1. Fix Trailers and semitrailers pricing. It's the only segment that never approaches breakeven in five years — raise premiums or tighten underwriting criteria here specifically.

2. Automobile, Bus, and Pick-up between 2016–2018 — their steady climb toward 100%+ is likely the real driver of the company's recovery.

3. Investigate Tractor's volatility - A swing from 154% to 3226% in one year is more consistent with a thin book exposed to one or two large claims than genuine pricing skill — consider reinsurance or a policy cap for tail risk here even though the average looks good.
